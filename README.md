# Isaac M. Thompson

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student in the
[MATRIX Machine Translation Lab](https://matrix.byu.edu/) at Brigham Young
University, advised by Dr. Eric Ringger. I study low-resource machine
translation, multilingual and cross-lingual evaluation, reward reliability,
and efficient LLM inference.

My work combines controlled experiments with research engineering: frozen
protocols, statistical audits, multilingual human judgments, QLoRA/LoRA
training, SLURM-scale model serving, and reproducible evaluation pipelines.

## Selected work

- [MT RL Policy Effects](https://github.com/imthomp/mt-rl-policy-effects):
  prospective audits of reward overoptimization in machine translation.
- [vLLM Batch Invariance](https://github.com/imthomp/vllm-batch-invariance):
  inference-correctness and kernel-performance studies with upstream vLLM
  contributions.
- [Right Answer, Wrong Language](https://github.com/imthomp/needle-in-a-haystack):
  an audit of span-F1 failures in cross-lingual QA evaluation.
- [Agentic MT](https://github.com/imthomp/agentic-mt): controlled FAISS
  retrieval, quality-estimation routing, and iterative MT refinement.
- [GLM Research Review](https://github.com/imthomp/glm-research-review):
  eight-GPU model serving and resumable multi-persona research review.
